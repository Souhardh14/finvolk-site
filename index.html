How it works

Four steps from first call to a steady monthly close.

STEP 1

Intro call

We learn how your business runs, which software you use and what is not working today. You get a clear scope.

STEP 2

Onboarding

Access to bank statements, software and documents. We review opening balances and fix the chart of accounts.

STEP 3

Monthly cycle

You share documents. We post, reconcile and close on an agreed date, and flag anything that needs your decision.

STEP 4

Review

A short walk-through of the reports, open items and next month. Advisory work is added when you ask for it.

Questions

Common questions

What do you need from me each month?

Bank and card statements, sales and purchase invoices, expense bills and any payroll or reimbursement sheets. We agree a list and a date during onboarding.

Which accounting software do you work in?

Tally Prime and Zoho Books. If you already use something else, tell us and we will confirm whether we can take it on.

Do you file GST, TDS or income tax returns?

Our core work is bookkeeping and reporting. We prepare the records so your CA or tax consultant can file. If you need filing handled, ask during the intro call and we will tell you how it can be arranged.

How is the fee decided?

Fees depend on transaction volume, number of bank accounts and the reports you need. You get a written quote after the intro call.

Can you clean up books that are months behind?

Yes. We review what exists, list the gaps, then catch up month by month and agree the scope with you before starting.

Is my financial information kept confidential?

Yes. We use your data only for your engagement and can sign a confidentiality agreement on request.
